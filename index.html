SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE

Engineered for Concurrent Hybrid Output

Combining high-mileage aerobic running with maximal vertical jump plyometrics creates severe neuromuscular interference if unmanaged. PLYOSTRIDE resolves the interference effect through six deterministic subsystems.

01 · NATIVE PERSISTENCE @capacitor-community/sqlite

3-Tier Native SQLite + OPFS Hardware Vault

Web-wrapper apps lose your training history the moment the mobile OS clears browser cache. PLYOSTRIDE writes directly to Android's physical flash storage (/data/data/com.plyostride.app/databases/plyostride_vault), backed by an automated Origin Private File System (OPFS) and persistent IndexedDB mirror. Export full .json snapshots, .csv spreadsheets, or transfer profiles across devices via zero-account Sync Codes.

02 · LOAD GOVERNANCE

ACWR & Keith Baar Tendon Gate

Tracks your 7-day Acute to 28-day Chronic Workload Ratio inside the 0.80–1.30 Sweet Spot. While muscle recovers in 24–48h, patellar and Achilles tendons require 48–72h. Logging tendon stiffness ≥ 4/10 automatically swaps high-impact Depth Jumps to 45-second analgesic isometric holds.

03 · MACROCYCLE ENGINE

52-Week Periodization & Race Reverse-Engineering

Progresses systematically from Zone 2 Aerobic Base & Tendon Armor (Months 1–3) to Force Load, Track Intervals ($6\times 800\text{m}$), and Championship Taper. Input any target 10K race date to reverse-engineer your current training week and auto-scale volume across Beginner (65%), Intermediate (85%), or Advanced (100%) tiers.

04 · NEURAL EXECUTION

2-Min PAP Primer & Hands-Free Player

Unlocks Post-Activation Potentiation (PAP) prior to max approach jumps. The full-screen workout player features hardware Screen WakeLock, automatic 5-second unilateral leg-switch transitions, and dual-frequency audio metronomes (180 SPM running cadence and 2.2 Hz / 132 BPM pogo stiffness rhythm).

05 · BIOMECHANICS & GEAR TELEMETRY EVA / PEBAX Foam Recovery

Midsole Foam Decompression & Slow-Mo Video GCT Scrubber

Running shoe midsoles require 24 hours to decompress after runs $\ge 10\text{ km}$. PLYOSTRIDE enforces shoe rotation between Zone 2 mileage trainers and carbon/tempo racers while counting cumulative jump contacts on your court shoes. Includes an on-board slow-motion video frame scrubber (60/120/240 FPS) to measure exact Ground Contact Time (GCT) and Reactive Strength Index (RSI).

DEPLOYMENT PROTOCOL

Installation & Native SQLite Verification

PLYOSTRIDE is distributed directly as an independent Android package to guarantee zero telemetry, zero paywalls, and unrestricted local filesystem access.

STEP 01

Download Release Binary

Tap the Download Stable APK button at the top of this page (or visit the GitHub Releases tab) to pull the latest .apk package to your Android device.

STEP 02

Authorize Direct Install

Open the downloaded APK. If prompted by Android security, tap Settings and toggle Allow from this source. On Google Play Protect prompts, tap More Details → Install Anyway.

STEP 03

Calibrate Athlete Tier

Launch PLYOSTRIDE and select your onboarding tier (Beginner 65%, Intermediate 85%, or Advanced 100%) to auto-calibrate all 52 weeks of running mileage, plyometric sets, and rest timers.

STEP 04

Verify SQLite Native Vault

Navigate to Data → System → App Icon Studio, Storage & Reset. Confirm the green SQLite Native ✓ · Auto-Vault ✓ indicator is active on your device hardware.
