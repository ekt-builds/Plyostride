Privacy Architecture, APK Security Audit & Biomechanical Disclaimer

Effective Date: November 2026 · Build Target: PLYOSTRIDE v1.0.0 Stable (com.plyostride.app)

1. Absolute Data Sovereignty (Zero-Cloud Architecture)

PLYOSTRIDE is engineered on a strict offline-first, local-execution architecture. Unlike commercial fitness platforms that monetize biometric telemetry or lock historical workout logs behind recurring cloud subscriptions, PLYOSTRIDE operates with zero external analytics servers, zero advertising SDKs, zero crash-reporting beacons, and zero background telemetry.

  • No Identity Harvesting: PLYOSTRIDE never requests, collects, or processes your name, email address, phone number, physical GPS route coordinates, IP address, or device advertising identifiers (GAID).
  • Multi-Profile Isolation: Athlete profiles created inside the app (e.g., Primary Athlete, Friend Profile) are isolated via deterministic key prefixes inside your local device storage and are never synced to any central directory.
  • Zero Silent Network Traffic: When you open PLYOSTRIDE, log a workout, calculate Reactive Strength Index (RSI), or scrub slow-motion jump footage, 0 bytes of network traffic are transmitted to PLYOSTRIDE developers.

2. On-Device SQLite Persistence & Sandbox Lifecycle

To guarantee resilience against mobile browser cache eviction while maintaining total privacy, PLYOSTRIDE writes your training state across a redundant 3-tier local storage hierarchy on your device:

  • Tier 1 — Native Android SQLite Vault: On physical Android devices running the compiled Capacitor APK, all state keys, biometric profiles, 52-week logs, and shoe odometers are committed directly to a native SQLite relational table located at /data/data/com.plyostride.app/databases/plyostride_vault via @capacitor-community/sqlite.
  • Tier 2 — Origin Private File System (OPFS) & IndexedDB Mirror: Every state mutation automatically writes an asynchronous snapshot (`plyostride_auto_vault.json`) to the hardware-backed Origin Private File System and IndexedDB (`PLYOSTRIDE_PERSISTENT_VAULT_DB`), requesting persistent OS storage allocation via navigator.storage.persist().
  • Tier 3 — User-Bound Auto-Save & Manual Exports: Users can export raw .json full-database backups, .csv training spreadsheets, clean schedule templates, or Base64 Sync Codes at any time from the Data tab.

CRITICAL DATA RETENTION WARNING (ANDROID OS SANDBOX): Because PLYOSTRIDE stores 100% of your data locally on your phone and maintains zero cloud copies, uninstalling the PLYOSTRIDE APK or selecting "Clear Storage / Clear Data" in Android System Settings will permanently destroy your internal SQLite database. Always use the Download .json or Export Log (.csv) buttons in the Data tab to save an external backup copy to your phone's Downloads folder or personal cloud drive before uninstalling or switching devices. When installing a newer APK version with the same signing key, install it directly over the existing app to preserve your SQLite database in place.

4. Optional Third-Party API Connections (BYOK Architecture)

PLYOSTRIDE features a built-in, deterministic Sports-Science Telemetry Advisor that runs 100% offline using local biomechanical rules (evaluating ACWR, RHR elevation, tendon pain gates, and shoe foam status).

  • Optional Google Gemini API Integration: If you voluntarily enter a personal Google Gemini API Key inside the Coach settings, your key is stored strictly inside your local device database. When you submit a prompt in Cloud Mode, your query and current workout context are transmitted directly from your device to Google's `generativelanguage.googleapis.com` endpoint under Google's API Terms of Service.
  • Optional Custom Cloud Vault Webhook: If you voluntarily enter a personal REST endpoint (such as a private Cloudflare Worker, Supabase instance, or self-hosted server), JSON sync payloads are sent exclusively to the URL you specified. PLYOSTRIDE developers never intercept, proxy, or have access to your keys or endpoints.

5. Clinical, Orthopedic & Biomechanical Assumption of Risk

PLYOSTRIDE IS AN ATHLETIC PERIODIZATION CALCULATOR AND TELEMETRY TRACKER, NOT A MEDICAL DEVICE OR LICENSED HEALTHCARE PROVIDER.

Maximal plyometric training (including Depth Jumps, Max Approach Vertical Jumps, Bounds, and Pogo Hops) combined with endurance distance running subjects the musculoskeletal and cardiovascular systems to extreme mechanical and metabolic stress—generating peak ground reaction forces of 5 to 8 times body weight across the patellar tendon, Achilles tendon, plantar fascia, tibial cortex, knee ligaments (ACL/MCL), and lumbosacral spine.

  • Mathematical Model Limitations: The Acute:Chronic Workload Ratio (ACWR 0.80–1.30 Sweet Spot), Keith Baar Isometric Tendon Analgesia Protocol (5×45s holds), Rathleff Plantar Loading, Flight-Time Physics ($h = g \cdot t^2 / 8$), Riegel Race Predictor ($T_2 = T_1 \cdot (D_2/D_1)^{1.06}$), and Karvonen Heart Rate Reserve (HRR) Zones are generalized sports-science formulas. They cannot detect subclinical collagen micro-ruptures, stress fractures, cardiac arrhythmias, or overtraining syndrome.
  • Mandatory Medical Clearance: Always consult a licensed physician, orthopedic surgeon, or physical therapist before beginning any high-impact plyometric, endurance running, or tendon rehabilitation regimen—especially if you have a history of Osgood-Schlatter disease, patellar/Achilles tendinopathy, shin splints (MTSS), plantar fasciitis, joint instability, or cardiovascular conditions.
  • Immediate Cessation Rule: Do not train through sharp, localized joint pain or acute tendon swelling. Stop exercising immediately if you experience sharp mechanical pain, dizziness, lightheadedness, chest tightness, or abnormal shortness of breath.
