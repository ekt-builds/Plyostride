LEGAL & DATA ARCHITECTURE

Privacy Policy & Clinical Disclaimer

Effective Date: November 2026 · Version 1.0.0 Stable

1. Absolute Data Sovereignty (Zero-Cloud Architecture)

PLYOSTRIDE is engineered on a strict offline-first, local-storage architecture. Unlike commercial fitness platforms, PLYOSTRIDE operates with zero external analytics servers, zero advertising SDKs, and zero telemetry tracking.

  • On-Device SQLite Persistence: All biometric inputs (height, weight, standing reach, Max/Resting Heart Rate), workout logs, weekly benchmarks, and shoe mileage records are written exclusively to your device's internal SQLite database (plyostride_vault), Origin Private File System (OPFS), and local IndexedDB vault.
  • No Account Required: You never register an email address, phone number, or password to use PLYOSTRIDE.
  • User-Controlled Portability: Your data leaves your device only when you explicitly trigger a .json backup, .csv training log export, Sync Code copy, or optional user-configured REST webhook/API request.

2. Optional Third-Party API Connections (BYOK)

PLYOSTRIDE includes a built-in deterministic sports-science advisor that functions 100% offline. If you optionally choose to enter a personal Google Gemini API Key or a custom Cloud Vault URL:

  • Your API key is stored strictly inside your local device database and is never transmitted to PLYOSTRIDE developers.
  • Requests are sent directly from your device to the endpoint you configured.

3. Hardware Permissions

  • Local Storage & Filesystem: Used strictly to read and write your training database backups (.json / .csv) and generated app icons (.png / .svg).
  • Screen WakeLock & Vibration: Used during active Guided Workout sessions, isometric holds, and cadence metronomes so your screen does not sleep mid-set.
  • Local Video File Input: When using the Slow-Mo Video GCT/RSI Frame Scrubber, video files are processed strictly in local memory via URL.createObjectURL and are never uploaded anywhere.

4. Clinical & Biomechanical Disclaimer

PLYOSTRIDE is an athletic periodization calculator and telemetry tracker, NOT a medical device.

High-intensity plyometrics (including Depth Jumps, Max Approach Jumps, and Pogo Hops) and endurance distance running place substantial mechanical forces (5–8× body weight) on the patellar tendon, Achilles tendon, plantar fascia, tibia, and central nervous system.

  • The ACWR (Acute:Chronic Workload Ratio), Keith Baar Isometric Tendon Analgesia Timer, Rathleff Plantar Protocol, and Karvonen Heart Rate Reserve (HRR) Zones are provided for informational and educational conditioning purposes only.
  • Always consult a qualified physician, orthopedic specialist, or licensed physical therapist before beginning any high-impact plyometric, endurance running, or tendon rehabilitation regimen.
  • Stop training immediately if you experience sharp joint pain, acute tendon swelling, dizziness, or chest discomfort.
