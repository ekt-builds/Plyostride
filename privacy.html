Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 17, 2026

1. Absolute Local Sovereignty

PLYOSTRIDE is engineered as an offline-first application. We do not operate cloud servers, databases, or remote telemetry systems. All data you generate—including biometrics, workout logs, GPS running distances, and subjective readiness scores—is written directly to a local SQLite database residing strictly on the internal storage of your device.

2. Data Collection and Transmission

We do not collect, harvest, transmit, or sell your data. Because the application has no backend cloud architecture, it is technically impossible for the developer to access your training history or health metrics.

3. Third-Party Analytics

PLYOSTRIDE does not embed third-party advertising SDKs, tracking pixels, or background analytics engines. Your training data is invisible to external marketing firms.

4. User Responsibility & Backups

Because your data is strictly localized, you are solely responsible for its preservation. If you uninstall the application, clear your device's application storage, or lose your phone, your data will be permanently destroyed. We provide raw `.json` and `.csv` export tools within the application. You are strongly advised to run manual backups to a secure personal location.

5. Medical Disclaimer

PLYOSTRIDE is an athletic periodization and biomechanical tracking tool. It is not a certified medical device. The telemetry provided (including ACWR and tendon resilience protocols) is based on standard sports science literature. It is not a substitute for professional medical diagnosis or physical therapy.

6. Contact

If you have questions regarding this offline architecture or need assistance with database extraction, submit an issue on the official GitHub repository.
