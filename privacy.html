Privacy Policy & Disclaimer

Effective Date: November 17, 2026

1. Absolute Local Sovereignty

PLYOSTRIDE is engineered as an offline-first application. We do not operate cloud servers, external databases, or remote telemetry systems. All data you generate—including biometrics, workout logs, running distances, shoe mileage, and readiness scores—is written directly to a local SQLite database residing strictly on your device.

2. Data Collection and Transmission

We do not collect, harvest, transmit, or sell your personal data. Because the application has no backend cloud architecture, it is technically impossible for the developer to access your training history or physiological metrics.

3. User Responsibility & Backups

Because your data is strictly localized, you are solely responsible for its preservation. If you uninstall the application, clear your device's application storage, or lose your phone, your data will be permanently erased. Use the System tab inside PLYOSTRIDE to export raw .json and .csv backups regularly.

4. Medical & Biomechanical Disclaimer

PLYOSTRIDE is an athletic periodization and biomechanical tracking tool, not a certified medical device. The telemetry provided (including ACWR, Karvonen HRR, and Arch/Tendon protocols) is based on standard sports science literature and is not a substitute for professional medical diagnosis or clinical physical therapy.

5. Contact & Support

If you have questions regarding this architecture or wish to report a bug, open an issue on the official GitHub repository.
